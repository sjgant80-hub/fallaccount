6 Common questions
What if my laptop breaks?
Tap 💾 Data & Export regularly. Save the backup file to email, Dropbox, or a USB stick. Open it on any new device.
Do I still need an accountant?
Most one-person businesses can do it themselves — this app does the maths. If you're complicated (limited company, lots of staff, VAT registered) keep your accountant. Hand them the export, they'll love you.
When's my tax due?
Online self-assessment by 31 January each year (for the tax year that ended the previous 5 April). From April 2026 you may also need to send quarterly summaries if you earn over £50k (the MTD Quarterly Reports tab handles that).
What can I actually claim?
Anything you bought "wholly and exclusively for the business". Tools, fuel for work trips, business mileage (45p/mile up to 10k miles), portion of phone, portion of home if you work from home, software, insurance, accountant fees, training. Open 📖 Tax Guide for the full list.
Is this legit?
It's built on the same UK tax rules as the expensive software. No magic. Just adds up your numbers and applies HMRC's bands and rates. Sense-check anything weird with HMRC's site or an accountant.
